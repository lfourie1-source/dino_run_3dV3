# Reference Items & Creative Direction

## Gameplay Reference
**Chrome Offline Dinosaur Game / “Chrome Dino”**

The project uses the basic conceptual reference of an endless runner: the player continuously advances, reacts to obstacles, jumps to survive, and attempts to increase a score. The final project does not copy Chrome Dino source code, sprites, audio, or exact level layouts. It changes the viewpoint to a behind-the-character perspective and adds lane movement, dimensions, coins, procedural patterns, and custom audio.

## Visual Direction
The final art direction was developed through iterative prompting rather than an imported art pack or external image set:
- Blocky / low-poly arcade styling.
- Small dinosaur viewed from behind.
- High-contrast black-and-white opening biome.
- Full-color desert second biome.
- Dark/white “void” third biome.
- Blocky rocks, cacti, full-width hurdles, coins, and road-line scenery.

No external image textures, sprites, or 3D models are used. Visuals are rendered procedurally with HTML Canvas drawing code.

## Gameplay Design References
- Endless-runner reaction loop: survive as long as possible.
- Arcade score chasing: score rises continuously; high score persists.
- Risk/reward pickups: coins add +10 score.
- Difficulty ramp: early game is forgiving and later play becomes faster and denser.
- Biome milestones: every 500 points changes the visual dimension and triggers a victory cue.

## Target Acoustic Style
- **Background:** energetic electronic/arcade loop that can repeat during continuous play.
- **Jump:** short, playful vocal/cartoon cue with minimal tail.
- **Death:** louder impact/failure cue that replaces the background music at game over.
- **Victory:** short, bright chime that clearly marks a biome transition without interrupting gameplay for long.
